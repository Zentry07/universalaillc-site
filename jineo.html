FAQ

Does Jineo store my face photo?
No. Selfies are sent to our processing server, analyzed by an AI vision model, and immediately discarded. We never retain the image.
Is Jineo medical advice?
No. Jineo is for K-beauty guidance and product matching. For skin conditions like persistent acne, eczema, or rosacea, see a licensed dermatologist.
Will it work on my skin tone?
Yes. The 12-tone Korean system is designed to span the full range of human skin tones. Our scanner is trained on a diverse dataset and refines weekly.
